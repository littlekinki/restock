Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Morwave ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information about users of the Restock mobile application and related services (the "Service").

By using Restock, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: Business name, owner name, phone number, email address, and password
  • Address information: Street address, city, state, and landmark for delivery purposes
  • Product information (distributors): Product names, prices, categories, stock levels
  • Payment information: Bank account details (bank name, account number, account name) for distributor payouts
  • Order information: Products ordered, quantities, delivery addresses, order history
  • Chat messages: Messages exchanged between shops, distributors, and riders within the Service

Information collected automatically:

  • Location data: For riders, we collect your GPS coordinates periodically (approximately every 10 seconds) while using the app, to enable real-time delivery tracking. Location is also used to determine delivery fees based on distance between shop and distributor.
  • Device information: Push notification tokens for delivering order updates
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the Service (orders placed, pages visited)

Information from third parties:

  • WhatsApp messages: If you place orders via our WhatsApp business number, we receive the phone number and message content you send
  • Map data: We use OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) to convert addresses into coordinates. Addresses you provide are sent to Nominatim to find geographic coordinates.

Camera and photo library access:

  • Our "Upload Shopping List" feature requires access to your camera or photo library. Images you upload are sent to our servers for OCR (text extraction) and are not stored permanently. Only the extracted product names are retained.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and fulfill orders between shops, distributors, and riders
  • Calculate delivery fees based on distance
  • Send SMS and push notifications about order status, chat messages, and account activity
  • Enable in-app chat between users in the same order
  • Track rider location in real time for delivery transparency
  • Process product requests and product listings
  • Improve the Service and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Between users of the Service: When a shop places an order, the distributor sees the shop's business name, phone number, and delivery address. When a rider is assigned, they see pickup and delivery addresses and phone numbers.
  • Service providers: We use the following third parties to operate the Service:
    • Render — for hosting our backend servers
    • MongoDB Atlas — for secure data storage
    • Termii — for sending SMS notifications and verification codes
    • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging — for delivering push notifications
    • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — for converting addresses to coordinates
    • Meta WhatsApp Business Platform — for the WhatsApp ordering feature
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request.

4. Data Retention

  • Account information: Retained for as long as your account is active. If you deactivate your account, we retain records for a limited period for legal and audit purposes.
  • Order history: Retained indefinitely for business analytics and dispute resolution.
  • Chat messages: Retained for as long as the related order exists.
  • In-app notifications: Automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Uploaded images: Deleted immediately after text extraction. Not stored.
  • Password reset codes: Auto-expire after 10 minutes.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher)
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we never store plain-text passwords
  • Database access is restricted to our backend servers
  • Authentication uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with 7-day expiry

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can view and download your account data from the app or by contacting us.
  • Correction: You can update your profile information at any time from the Settings screen.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account by contacting us. We will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
  • Opt-out of notifications: You can disable SMS and push notifications from Settings → Notification Preferences.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Restock account and associated data:

  1. Email us at restock@morwaveng.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the phone number associated with your account
  3. We will process your request within 30 days

Data that will be deleted: Your profile information (name, phone, address), your order history, and your chat messages.

Data that will be retained: Transaction records required for legal and tax purposes (retained for 7 years).

7. Children's Privacy

Restock is a business-to-business service intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Nigeria (including the United States, where our hosting providers operate). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy and updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Third-Party Links

The Service may contain links to third-party websites (such as WhatsApp or bank payment pages). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.
